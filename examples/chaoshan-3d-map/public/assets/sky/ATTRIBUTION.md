# Sky photography

Two unmodified 2K HDR photographs from Poly Haven:

- https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_03_puresky
- https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky

Original photography: Greg Zaal. Sky edits: Jarod Guest.
License: CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license).

Source file checksums are recorded in photographic-sky.mjs.
The renderer applies soft color grading, photographic sun tracking and simulated drift.
These are not photographs of Chaoshan or a live local weather feed.
