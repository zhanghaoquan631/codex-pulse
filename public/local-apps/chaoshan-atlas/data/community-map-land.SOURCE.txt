Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, public domain.
Downloaded 2026-09-26 from the upstream Natural Earth Vector repository:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Source and terms:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/110m-land/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The file is stored without modification as community-map-land.geojson.
It provides a coarse coastline fallback under interactive street tiles.
No administrative borders are drawn. It is not suitable for navigation or surveying.

OpenStreetMap tiles are requested on demand from tile.openstreetmap.org;
they are not prefetched or bulk downloaded. Attribution is visible in the map.
Community coordinates remain exactly as provided by the BetterOPC snapshot;
the source coordinate reference system is unknown.
